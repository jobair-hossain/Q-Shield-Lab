RESEARCH AREA 01

Health Informatics

Computational oncology, digital pathology & clinical intelligence

Related papers ↗

Under what data and uncertainty conditions can AI and hybrid quantum–classical methods improve histopathology analysis and clinical decision support relative to established approaches?

Our work progresses from deep learning for digital pathology to hybrid quantum–classical tumor analysis, adaptive fusion, and multimodal clinical decision support.

01 Published research

Deep Learning for Digital Pathology and Tumor Characterization

Research question. Which deep-learning architectures provide reliable binary and multi-class tumor characterization under patient-separated histopathology and whole-slide evaluation?

Approach. Investigate convolutional networks and vision transformers for tumor classification from image tiles to whole-slide inference, with class-wise error analysis and patient-level validation.

Publications
  • Deep Learning-Based Multi-Class Analysis of Whole-Slide Images IEEE/ACM CHASE · 2025 ↗

02 Published research

Hybrid Quantum–Classical Tumor Analysis and Adaptive Fusion

Research question. Which quantum encodings, optimization strategies, and adaptive fusion policies improve tumor classification and reliability relative to matched classical and fixed-fusion baselines?

Approach. Integrate deep image features with parameterized quantum circuits, evaluate quantum optimizers, and investigate reinforcement-learning-guided weighting of classical and quantum experts.

Publications

  • Reinforcement Learning-Guided Adaptive Fusion for Tumor Classification IEEE ICHI · 2026 ↗

03 Ongoing research

Trustworthy Multimodal Clinical Intelligence and Generative AI

Research question. To what extent do evidence grounding, multimodal integration, and uncertainty-aware model consensus improve diagnostic reliability and clinician reviewability under incomplete or conflicting clinical evidence?

Approach. Investigate multimodal pathology and clinical-data interpretation, model consensus, traceable evidence, and uncertainty-aware generation, with clinician oversight and abstention when support is insufficient.

RESEARCH AREA 02

Quantum AI

Mathematical foundations, quantum learning & data representation

Related papers ↗

What can resource-constrained quantum representations preserve, and how do encoding, optimization, and noise affect learning compared with well-matched classical methods?

We investigate mathematical limits of quantum representations, trainable hybrid models, adaptive learning, and quantum-ready data for histopathology and cybersecurity. Evaluations account for qubits, circuit depth, measurement cost, noise, and classical baselines.

01 Research direction

Mathematical Foundations of Structure-Preserving Quantum Representation Learning for Multiscale Histopathology Images

Research question. Under what mathematical conditions can quantum encodings preserve task-relevant structure from cells to whole-slide tissue organization, and how do representation distortion and quantum resources constrain trainability and generalization?

Approach. Model multiscale histopathology using hierarchical tensors and multiscale graphs. Derive task-aware distortion measures, resource–distortion bounds, and learning guarantees that account for qubits, circuit depth, finite measurements, and quantum noise; characterize when classical approximations are sufficient.

Multiscale representationQuantum encoding theoryResource–distortion bounds
02 Published foundation

Quantum Encodings and Optimization for Hybrid Learning

Research question. Which encoding and optimization choices improve the trainability and predictive performance of parameterized quantum models under fixed circuit and measurement budgets?

Approach. Evaluate feature encodings, parameterized circuits, and optimizers on shared data splits, with attention to convergence, noise sensitivity, resource requirements, and comparable classical models.

Publications
  • Hybrid Classical–Quantum Machine Learning for Tumor Mutation Classification IEEE ICHI · 2026 ↗
  • Evolution of Quantum Computing: A Systematic Survey on the Use of Quantum Computing Tools IEEE COMPSAC · 2022 ↗
Hybrid QMLParameterized circuitsQuantum optimization
03 Published foundation

Adaptive and Robust Quantum–Classical Learning

Research question. When does adaptive quantum–classical fusion outperform individual models and fixed fusion, and how does its behavior change under data shift, finite-shot noise, and unreliable predictions?

Approach. Study learned weighting of classical and quantum models, including reinforcement-learning policies, and evaluate generalization, calibration, noise sensitivity, and stability against matched baselines.

Publications
  • Reinforcement Learning-Guided Adaptive Quantum–Classical Fusion IEEE ICHI · 2026 ↗
Adaptive fusionReinforcement learningRobustness
04 Research direction

Quantum-Ready Cybersecurity and Healthcare Datasets

Research question. Which source-data properties, transformations, and quantum encodings retain attack-related or clinically relevant information under realistic quantum-resource limits, while allowing other researchers to reproduce the representations and benchmarks?

Approach. Develop Q-Data specifications for source semantics, provenance, preprocessing, encodings, hardware profiles, partitions, and baseline evaluation. The initial cybersecurity work examines NIST TrojAI poisoning data; a separate healthcare extension will address patient-level separation, clinical meaning, access controls, and data governance.

RESEARCH AREA 03

Cybersecurity

Adversarial AI, cyber defense & secure scientific computing

Related papers ↗

Which learning methods and access-control mechanisms hold up against evolving cyber threats, privacy attacks, and unauthorized use of scientific data?

Our work includes quantum learning for malware and software supply chains, adversarial and privacy attacks on AI, intrusion detection, and privacy-preserving access to scientific computing resources.

01 Published foundation

Quantum-AI for Malware and Software Supply-Chain Security

Research question. Which quantum feature representations and learning architectures support reliable detection of malware and software supply-chain threats under resource limits and previously unseen conditions?

Approach. Evaluate quantum neural networks and hybrid classifiers for malware and vulnerable-code analysis, comparing against tuned classical models and extending evaluation toward unseen threats.

Publications

QML for cybersecurityMalware analysisSoftware supply chain
02 Published foundation

Adversarial Resilience and Privacy of AI Systems

Research question. Which attack and defense conditions determine the effectiveness of prompt-injection, poisoning, and model-manipulation safeguards while limiting private-information leakage and false alarms?

Approach. Study prompt-injection detection and model-inversion attacks, with ongoing evaluation of adversarial perturbations, data poisoning, unseen attacks, and uncertainty-aware defenses.

Publications
  • Fine-Tuned Large Language Models for Prompt-Injection Attack Detection IEEE COMPSAC · 2025 ↗
  • Exposing Privacy Vulnerabilities in Federated Learning: A GAN-Based Model Inversion Attack IEEE BigData · 2025 ↗
Adversarial AIModel privacyUnseen threats
03 Published research

Verifiable Credentials and Secure Scientific Data Access

Research question. What security, privacy, and trust trade-offs arise when verifiable credentials and smart contracts are used to authenticate users and authorize access to shared scientific data, compared with token-based approaches such as SciTokens?

Approach. Study SciTokens, verifiable credentials, and smart-contract access control. Implement a blockchain-backed credential framework, examine Ethereum and Hyperledger Fabric, and evaluate authorized data sharing and the security properties of each approach.

Publications
  • Blockchain-Based Decentralized Verifiable Credentials for Scientific Data Access IEEE BigData · 2023 ↗

04 Published research

Machine Learning for Network and IoT Intrusion Detection

Research question. Which model-selection and training strategies improve intrusion detection across network and IoT data, including non-identically distributed clients, without compromising privacy or incurring excessive computation and communication costs?

Approach. Investigate Bayesian optimization of deep intrusion detectors and random-projection-based personalized federated learning for distributed IoT monitoring; evaluate detection, privacy exposure, and computational requirements.

RESEARCH AREA 04

Software Engineering

AI for software engineering & engineering emerging systems

Related papers ↗

How should software engineering methods adapt to AI and blockchain systems, and where can AI measurably improve software development, testing, and maintenance?

We study AI for software engineering, software processes and assurance for emerging technologies, empirical computing education, and accessible intelligent applications.

01 Published foundation

AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)

Research question. To what extent can AI-augmented software development processes improve requirements quality, architectural consistency, development efficiency, and testing effectiveness while maintaining reliability, security, and human oversight?

Approach. Investigate how AI can be integrated into modern SDLC practices, including Agile, DevOps, and DevSecOps, across requirements engineering, software architecture and design, implementation, testing, and maintenance. Develop and evaluate AI-assisted engineering workflows, requirements traceability, code analysis, and verification practices for conventional and AI-enabled software systems.

Publications
  • AI-Oriented Software Engineering: Challenges, Opportunities and New Directions Springer · 2023 ↗
AI4SE AI-Augmented SDLC Requirements Engineering Software Architecture Software Testing
02 Published research

Software Engineering for Emerging Technologies

Research question. Which established requirements, architecture, development-process, testing, and security practices need to change for blockchain-based and AI-enabled software?

Approach. Study limitations of conventional software processes for smart contracts, decentralized applications, and AI-based components. Derive and assess guidance for requirements, architecture, verification, secure coding, and maintenance from systematic reviews and software case studies.

Publications
  • Project-Based Learning in Software Engineering with Blockchain-Oriented Repositories IEEE/ACIS SERA · 2024 ↗
  • Software Engineering Process and Methodology in Blockchain-Oriented Software Development IEEE/ACIS SERA · 2022 ↗
Software processesBlockchain & Web3Engineering AI systems
