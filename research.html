RESEARCH AREA 01

Health Informatics

Computational oncology, digital pathology & clinical intelligence

Related papers ↗

How can we make computational analysis of complex clinical evidence more reliable, transparent, and useful for clinicians?

Our work progresses from deep learning for digital pathology to hybrid quantum–classical tumor analysis, adaptive fusion, and multimodal clinical decision support.

01Foundational research

Binary Tumor Classification from Histopathology

Deep-learning models for binary tumor-analysis tasks using digitized histopathology. The work examines image representations, classification reliability, and the transition from image tiles to whole-slide interpretation.

Deep learningWhole-slide imaging
02Published research

Multi-Class Histopathology Analysis

Vision transformer-based analysis of whole-slide images to distinguish tissue and cellular categories, including cancer, tumor, mitosis, red blood cells, and karyorrhexis.

Vision transformersMulti-class classification
03Published research

Hybrid Classical–Quantum Tumor Analysis

Integrating classical visual features with parameterized quantum learning models for histopathological classification, with systematic evaluation of quantum optimizers.

Hybrid QMLQuantum optimization
04Published research

Reinforcement Learning-Guided Quantum–Classical Fusion

Adaptive fusion of classical and quantum model predictions using reinforcement learning to learn decision weights rather than relying on fixed model combinations.

Adaptive fusionReinforcement learning
05Ongoing research

Evidence-Grounded Generative AI for Clinical Decision Support

Investigating model consensus, evidence traceability, and uncertainty-aware generation so clinical interpretations can be examined against their supporting information.

Generative AIClinical reasoning
06Research direction

Multimodal Clinical Intelligence

Integrating pathology, radiology, genomics, laboratory results, electronic health records, and clinical narratives while accounting for missing data, conflicting evidence, and uncertainty.

Multimodal AIEvidence provenance

RESEARCH AREA 02

Quantum AI

Cross-cutting learning methods & optimization

Related papers ↗

How should quantum representations, learning architectures, and optimization be designed and evaluated for trustworthy AI?

Rather than treating quantum AI as a separate application, we study the methods that connect our cybersecurity and health-informatics research.

01Research direction

Structure-Preserving Quantum Representation Learning

Studying quantum encodings and feature representations that retain task-relevant structure across high-dimensional biomedical and cybersecurity data.

Quantum encodingRepresentation learning
02Ongoing research

Trainable and Resource-Aware Hybrid Quantum Models

Investigating parameterized circuits, classical–quantum interfaces, optimizer choice, noise sensitivity, and trainability under realistic qubit and circuit-depth constraints.

Parameterized circuitsOptimization
03Published foundation

Adaptive Quantum–Classical Learning and Fusion

Building on published reinforcement-learning-guided fusion methods to investigate how quantum and classical components can be selected and weighted in response to evidence.

Model fusionHybrid learning
04Research direction

Robustness, Uncertainty, and Generalization in QML

Evaluating quantum-AI systems under distribution shifts, adversarial manipulation, and noise, with calibration and held-out tests alongside strong classical baselines.

RobustnessUncertainty
05Research direction

Quantum-Ready Datasets and Reproducible Evaluation

Developing reusable data preparation, encoding documentation, benchmarking protocols, and resource-aware comparisons for quantum-AI cybersecurity research.

BenchmarkingReproducibility

RESEARCH AREA 03

Cybersecurity

Adversarial resilience & secure scientific computing

Related papers ↗

How can we defend AI-enabled systems, software dependencies, and scientific data against evolving threats?

We investigate quantum-AI cyber defense, software supply-chain security, privacy-preserving authentication, and the integrity of research workflows.

01Published research

Quantum Machine Learning for Software Supply-Chain Security

Applying classical and quantum machine learning to source-code vulnerability and software supply-chain attack analysis, including comparative study of their detection behavior.

Software supply chainQML
02Published research

Quantum Neural Networks for Malware Classification

Investigating quantum neural networks for malware detection and classification, with attention to feature representation and feasible circuit-based classifiers.

Malware analysisQNN
03Research direction

Adversary-Resilient Quantum AI for Cyber Defense

Advancing defenses against adversarial inputs, training-data poisoning, model manipulation, and previously unseen threats, with emphasis on robustness beyond familiar attack families.

Adversarial AIUnseen threats
04Published research

Verifiable Credentials for Secure Scientific Data Access

A blockchain-based decentralized credential framework using smart contracts for privacy-preserving authentication, authorization, and verifiable access to scientific data.

Verifiable credentialsSmart contracts
05Published foundation

Security of AI Models and Scientific Workflows

Extending published research on prompt-injection detection and federated-learning privacy attacks toward model integrity, artifact provenance, and trustworthy scientific cyberinfrastructure.

AI securityProvenance

RESEARCH AREA 04

Software Engineering

Engineering AI systems & research-based learning

Related papers ↗

How can software engineering evolve for AI-enabled systems while using AI to improve software quality and education?

We connect AI for software engineering (AI4SE) with engineering for AI systems, software quality, accessibility, and authentic project-based learning.

01Published research

AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)

CodePulse combines LLM-based bug detection with CodeBERT validation for Python source-code analysis, illustrating research on AI-assisted software quality and defect detection.

CodePulseAI-assisted analysis
02Published foundation

Software Engineering for AI-Enabled Systems

Advancing AI-oriented software engineering through requirements, architecture, verification, reliability, human oversight, and responsible development practices for systems that include learning components.

SE for AITrustworthy systems
03Published research

Project-Based Software Engineering Education

Research-integrated learning that connects software engineering coursework with emerging technologies, including blockchain-oriented repositories and student-led project development.

Project-based learningEmerging technologies
04Published student-led research

Accessible and Human-Centered Intelligent Software

CursorVision investigates real-time gaze-controlled interaction for accessible STEM learning and educational gaming, linking AI, interface design, and student research.

Accessible computingHuman–AI interaction
