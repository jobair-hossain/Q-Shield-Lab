Welcome to Q-SHIELD Lab

Advancing trustworthy quantum-AI and secure software engineering for health informatics, cybersecurity, and emerging defense.

Q-SHIELD Lab advances artificial intelligence (AI) and quantum machine learning (QML) to develop reliable, secure, and trustworthy intelligent systems. Our research spans two primary application domains: cybersecurity, where we investigate adversarial and emerging threats, model manipulation, software supply-chain security, and intelligent cyber defense; and health informatics, where we advance computational pathology, multimodal medical data analysis, and clinical decision support. Across these domains, we study representation learning, optimization, robustness, uncertainty, and trustworthy decision-making under practical computational constraints. Complementing this work, our software engineering research develops methods and practices for designing, building, testing, securing, and evaluating dependable AI-enabled and classical–quantum software systems.
