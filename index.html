Welcome to Q-SHIELD Lab

Advancing trustworthy quantum-AI and secure software engineering for health informatics, cybersecurity, and emerging defense.

Q-SHIELD Lab advances trustworthy artificial intelligence, quantum machine learning, and secure software systems for challenging problems in health informatics and cybersecurity, while training undergraduate and graduate students through interdisciplinary, research-driven projects.

Our research spans digital pathology, multimodal medical data analysis, and clinical decision support; adversarially robust AI and quantum-AI for cybersecurity; and secure scientific computing, including software supply-chain security, secure data access, privacy-aware authentication, and cyberinfrastructure protection. We also investigate the software engineering foundations of quantum-AI systems, including requirements, architecture, testing, security, and lifecycle practices for classical–quantum software. Across these areas, our goal is to develop intelligent systems that are reliable, transparent, secure, and practical for real-world deployment.
