Welcome to Q-SHIELD Lab

Exploring trustworthy quantum machine learning and intelligent software that advance health informatics and strengthen emerging defense

Q-SHIELD Lab’s mission is to advance trustworthy artificial intelligence and quantum machine learning that address real problems in healthcare and cybersecurity, while training the next generation of interdisciplinary researchers at the University of Central Arkansas.

We believe that today’s hardest problems — securing clinical AI, protecting research cyberinfrastructure, and preparing a quantum-ready workforce — sit at the intersection of multiple disciplines. Q-SHIELD Lab pursues a long-term research vision spanning health informatics, quantum machine learning, cybersecurity & emerging defense, and intelligent, accessible software — informed by digital pathology, hybrid quantum–classical modeling, secure systems design, and human-centered computing education.
