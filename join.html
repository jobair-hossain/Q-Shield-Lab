Join the Q-SHIELD Lab

Research opportunities for undergraduate and graduate students

Undergraduate and graduate students interested in research are encouraged to contact Q-SHIELD Lab in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Central Arkansas (UCA), under the supervision of Dr. Md Jobair Hossain Faruk.

🔬 Research Areas

Students will engage in research at the intersection of computer science, quantum computing, and emerging technologies, focusing on:

Health informatics & digital pathology

Quantum machine learning

Cybersecurity & secure cyberinfrastructure

Intelligent, accessible software

Research Opportunities & Qualifications

Students with interests in AI, quantum machine learning, cybersecurity, health informatics, or software engineering may inquire about research collaboration. Participation and financial support depend on current projects, supervision capacity, and available funding.

Undergraduate Researchers

  • Currently enrolled in computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, data science, or a related undergraduate program.
  • Motivation to learn, collaborate, and contribute to research projects.
  • Some experience with programming (Python or a similar language) or a clear willingness to build those skills.
  • Prior research publications are not required.

Graduate Researchers

  • Relevant B.S./B.Sc., M.S./M.Sc., or enrollment in a relevant graduate program.
  • Research interests aligned with Q-SHIELD projects and readiness for independent technical work.
  • Programming and research-methods experience relevant to the proposed project.
  • Prior publications or experience with Qiskit, PennyLane, deep learning, or security are beneficial but not mandatory.

For all applicants: Clear communication, research integrity, and willingness to work in an interdisciplinary team are important.

📝 How to Apply

1

Check the fit

Review the research areas and qualifications above and make sure your interests align.

2

Prepare your materials

Undergraduates: a resume and a brief paragraph about your research interests. Graduate applicants: a CV and a one-page description of interests, relevant skills, and potential research questions. References may be requested later.

3

Send a personal email

Email your materials directly — avoid generic templates.

Apply for a Research Position at Q-SHIELD Lab

We welcome thoughtful inquiries from students at different stages of research preparation. An inquiry does not guarantee an available or funded position.

Email Subject: Prospective Researcher — [Your Program/Semester]

Send Your Application to: mhossainfaruk@uca.edu

Tip: Briefly explain which Q-SHIELD project interests you, what experience you bring, and what you hope to learn.

🎓 Apply for Admission at UCA

Applicants must meet UCA’s Department of Computer Science and Engineering admission requirements for the B.S. or M.S. in Computer Science.
