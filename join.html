Join The Q-SHIELD Lab

Graduate and undergraduate research positions available

Graduate and undergraduate research positions are available at Q-SHIELD Lab in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Central Arkansas (UCA), under the supervision of Dr. Md Jobair Hossain Faruk.

🔬 Research Areas

Students will engage in research at the intersection of computer science, quantum computing, and emerging technologies, focusing on:

  • Health informatics & digital pathology
  • Quantum machine learning
  • Cybersecurity & secure cyberinfrastructure
  • Intelligent, accessible software & human-centered computing

📋 Required Qualifications

Educational Background

  • B.S./B.Sc. or M.S./M.Sc. in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, or related fields

Skills & Competencies

  • Strong motivation and passion for interdisciplinary research
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Proficiency in Python or a similar programming language

Preferred Skills (Not Mandatory, but a Plus!)

  • Experience with quantum computing frameworks (Qiskit, PennyLane), deep learning, medical imaging, blockchain, or human–computer interaction

📝 Apply for a Research Position at Q-SHIELD Lab

We encourage you to apply only if you meet the qualifications and share our research interests!

Email Subject: Prospective Researcher — [Your Program/Semester]

Send Your Application to: mhossainfaruk@uca.edu

Application Materials (must include for a review & response)

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV) — include names and contact details of two to three academic/professional references
  • Research Summary (1–2 pages) — outline your previous research and accomplishments
  • Research Statement (1–2 pages) — describe your long-term research goals and how they align with Q-SHIELD Lab

📝 Tip: Avoid generic email templates. A well-crafted, personalized email and the above documents will increase your chances of receiving a response!

🎓 Apply for Admission at UCA

Applicants must meet UCA’s Department of Computer Science and Engineering admission requirements for the B.S. or M.S. in Computer Science.
